# The Systematic Edge — Chương 27 (ML4T)

## Summary

Chương 27 là chương kết luận: **process is the durable edge, not any single strategy**. 5-Stage ML4T Workflow được xem như alpha factory blueprint: chống cognitive biases bằng falsifiable hypotheses, out-of-sample testing nghiêm ngặt, và statistical corrections cho multiple testing.

Chương này chỉ có README.md, không có notebook/code.

### Sections (5)

1. **27.1 Systematic Edge** — từ technique sang philosophy; process > strategy
2. **27.2 Modern Quant Career** — 5 archetypes: researcher, trader, developer, PM, risk manager; quantamental trend
3. **27.3 Learning Arsenal** — canonical texts, blogs/arXiv/aggregators, tools-by-category, community/brand building
4. **27.4 Frontiers** — quantum, DeFi, ethical AI; pragmatic attention allocation
5. **27.5 Strategic Path Forward** — skills assessment, PKM, accountability, burnout risk, career failure modes

### Key Takeaways

- Durable edge comes from a repeatable, falsifiable, monitored process.
- Quant career success needs T-shaped expertise: deep primary skill + broad lifecycle understanding.
- DeFi is actionable now; quantum is monitor-only; AI ethics/auditability is now compliance, not philosophy.
- Burnout is a professional risk because fatigue increases susceptibility to the same biases systematic processes fight.
- Avoid four career traps: over-specialization, ignoring soft skills, ignoring regulatory change, and perpetual learning without application.
