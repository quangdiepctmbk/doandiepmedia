# Strategy Map and Sources of Edge

**§6.2** — Phân loại ý tưởng trước khi xây model.

## Strategy Families (bộ lọc khả thi)
- Price-Based (momentum, mean-reversion)
- Fundamental (value, quality)
- Structural (carry, volatility)
- ML-Enhanced (factor combo, regime-switching)

## Sources of Edge (bộ lọc bền vững)
- Risk premium (compensation for bearing risk)
- Behavioral bias (systematic mispricing)
- Structural constraint (limits to arbitrage)
- Information advantage (access/speed)

## Failure Modes
- Data mining / p-hacking
- Capacity constraints
- Regime change
- Competition erosion (McLean & Pontiff 2016)
