# Quant Frontiers: Quantum, DeFi, Ethical AI

Chương 27 gives pragmatic attention allocation across frontier technologies.

## Quantum Computing

- Still in NISQ era
- Meaningful financial advantage likely mid-2030s earliest
- Worth monitoring, not immediate priority for most practitioners

## DeFi

- Actionable alpha opportunities today
- On-chain data, AMM optimization, yield farming
- Risks: smart contract vulnerabilities, liquidity fragility, regulatory uncertainty

## Ethical / Auditable AI

- No longer philosophy only — becoming compliance requirement
- EU AI Act and high-risk financial AI require explainability and auditability
- Practitioners need interpretability, bias detection, robustness testing, and governance literacy
